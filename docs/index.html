CyberSid privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

CyberSid is a free app that shows short summaries of cyber security news, with links to the original articles. This policy explains what information the app uses. In short: you do not need an account, and we only store the few details needed for the optional alerts and email digest you choose to switch on.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any account details. We do not use advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Stories you bookmark ("Saved"), which stories you have already seen (for the "New" labels), and the products you pick in Preferences are stored on your phone. They are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Phone alerts (optional)

If you allow notifications, the app creates an anonymous push address for your phone. We store that address together with the products and keywords you picked, only so we can send you at most one alert an hour about news that matters to you. It does not include your name, number or anything else about you. Alerts are delivered by Expo and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. Turn alerts off in Preferences (or in your phone's settings) and your push address is deleted from our list; it is also removed automatically if you uninstall the app.

Daily email (optional)

If you subscribe to the daily email, we store your email address and the products and keywords you picked, only to send you that email. Nothing is sent until you click the confirmation link we email you. Every email has an unsubscribe link, which deletes your email address from our list straight away. We never share or sell your email address. Emails are sent through Google's Gmail service.

Error reports

If you tap the flag icon to report a problem with a story, the app sends only the story's ID and the reason you picked (for example "Wrong facts"). It does not include anything that identifies you.

How the app gets news

News cards are loaded from our database, hosted by Supabase. Like any website or app that loads content over the internet, the hosting provider may briefly process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the content securely. We do not use this information to identify you.

News content and links

Summaries are written automatically from public news articles, with a link to the original source. Article images are shown with credit to the publishing website. When an article has no suitable picture, the card shows an illustration made by AI, clearly marked "AI illustration". When you open a link, you leave the app and the other website's privacy policy applies.

Children

CyberSid is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

About CyberSid

CyberSid is an independent app based in the United Kingdom. Each news card names its original publisher and links to the full article, and shows when it was published. Tip and explainer cards are written by CyberSid and shown with "CyberSid Tips" as the source.
